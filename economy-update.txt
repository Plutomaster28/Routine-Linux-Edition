# Economy Bot v2 – Economic Simulation Expansion


## Core Philosophy


The economy should not be scripted. It should emerge from player behavior.


Instead of players simply earning and spending money, their actions should influence confidence, investment, inflation, and economic health. Prosperity and recessions should be natural consequences of the simulation rather than manually triggered events.


---


# Global Player Data + Local Economies


## Player Data (Global)


Player data is shared across every server.


Examples:


* Account
* Profile
* Achievements
* Education
* Licenses
* Reputation
* Global collectibles
* Long-term statistics


This allows players to move between servers while keeping their identity.


---


## Server Economy (Local)


Each server maintains its own economy.


Examples:


* Currency
* Inflation
* Interest rates
* Stock market
* Banks
* Commodity prices
* Jobs
* Salaries
* Taxes
* Government debt
* Consumer confidence
* Economic health


This allows every server to naturally develop into its own economy.


---


# Foreign Exchange (Forex)


Since economies are local, currencies are separate.


Players can exchange currencies between servers.


Example:


```
Server A Currency
        ↓
 Foreign Exchange
        ↓
Server B Currency
```


Exchange rates should fluctuate based on supply and demand.


Potential mechanics:


* Exchange fees
* Spread between buy/sell prices
* Currency appreciation/depreciation
* International investment


---


# Capital Flight


When a server performs poorly, players may move wealth elsewhere.


Possible behavior:


* Exchange local currency
* Transfer investments
* Move savings
* Buy foreign assets


Large migrations should naturally weaken the original economy.


This creates realistic market behavior without scripting it.


---


# Economic Confidence


Confidence should influence spending.


High confidence:


* More investing
* More spending
* Business expansion
* Hiring increases


Low confidence:


* Saving increases
* Spending decreases
* Investment falls
* Layoffs increase


Confidence itself can create economic cycles.


---


# Player-Created Recessions


Players should be capable of causing recessions through panic.


Example chain:


```
Rumor spreads


↓


Players sell assets


↓


Currency weakens


↓


Confidence falls


↓


Businesses earn less


↓


Layoffs begin


↓


Consumer spending decreases


↓


Recession
```


The recession is not scripted.


The players created it.


---


# Recovery Mechanics


The economy should also recover naturally.


Possible recovery systems:


* Lower interest rates
* Government stimulus
* Infrastructure spending
* Strong corporate earnings
* Consumer confidence returning
* Increased hiring
* Long-term undervalued investments attracting buyers


The goal is recovery without manually resetting the economy.


---


# Local vs Global News


## Local News


Only affects one server.


Example:


> Fire damages Rat Mining facility.


Effects:


* Local mining sector falls
* Local confidence decreases
* Local stocks react


Other servers remain mostly unaffected.


---


## Global News


Affects every connected server.


Example:


> MEOW unveils the PlayStation 2.


Effects:


* Electronics sector rises globally
* Consumer confidence increases
* Technology companies gain value


Every server reacts.


---


# News Progression


Some events can evolve.


Example:


```
Local Discovery


↓


Regional Growth


↓


Global Export


↓


Worldwide Economic Boom
```


A local success can eventually become international news.


---


# Positive Economic Events


Positive events should exist just as often as negative ones over long periods.


## Common


Small improvements.


Examples:


* Good harvest
* Better-than-expected earnings
* Shipping delays resolved
* Small interest rate reduction


---


## Uncommon


Noticeable improvements.


Examples:


* Rat Mining discovers new mineral deposit
* Government infrastructure investment
* New successful product launch


---


## Rare


Major economic changes.


Examples:


* MEOW releases the PlayStation 2
* Revolutionary AI breakthrough
* Major trade agreement
* Historic manufacturing innovation


---


## Legendary


Generation-defining events.


Examples:


* Commercial fusion power
* Revolutionary computing breakthrough
* Massive technological revolution


These should be remembered for months.


---


# Negative Events


Examples:


* Factory fires
* Banking panic
* Commodity shortages
* Trade embargoes
* Corporate scandals
* Shipping disruptions
* Natural disasters
* Major wars


These should lower confidence and negatively affect affected industries.

# Market Speculation


Markets should react to expectations.


Example:


```
Rumor:
"MEOW preparing major announcement."


↓


Stock rises.


↓


Announcement Day.


↓


Good announcement
Stock rises further.


OR


Disappointing announcement
Stock crashes.
```


Players should speculate before information becomes public.


---


# International Trade


Future expansion.


Possible mechanics:


* Imports
* Exports
* Shipping
* Tariffs
* Trade agreements
* Resource shortages
* Manufacturing chains


Server economies become interconnected.


---


# Economic Stabilizers


Prevent permanent collapse.


Possible mechanics:


* Central bank intervention
* Emergency lending
* Deposit insurance
* Government stimulus
* Bond issuance
* Interest rate changes


These reduce the chance of endless economic decline while still allowing recessions.


---

Examples:

* Slow growth


---

* Large rewards

## Chaotic Economy

* Huge stock swings

These identities should emerge from player behavior rather than presets.


---


# Long-Term Goal


The economy should become a living system where:


* Players influence markets.
* Markets influence players.
* News changes investment behavior.
* Confidence creates booms and recessions.
* Every server develops its own financial identity.
* Global events connect the entire world.
* The best stories are created by players, not scripted by the bot.


The objective is to build an economy that players remember because **they accidentally caused history**, whether that history was a historic boom, a devastating recession, or a panic caused by someone posting "Bank of Miyamii is cooked" in #general.
